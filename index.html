You can select your Scheme (2024 or 2019), Engineering Branch, and Semester above to instantly view and download question papers for all regular and supplementary university examination sessions.
Are 2024 Scheme KTU question papers and syllabus included? expand_more
Yes! We actively maintain and index the latest 2024 Scheme curriculum copies, module-wise syllabus, model question papers, and semester exam papers as released by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University.
Are the solved question papers verified with official answer keys? expand_more
All solved question papers and scheme answer keys featured on the portal are verified against official KTU valuation schemes and prepared by experienced engineering faculty members.

This portal is an initiative developed and actively maintained by ELECSTA (Electronics and Communication Students' Technical Association), College of Engineering Karunagappally (CEK) to assist university engineering students across all branches.
